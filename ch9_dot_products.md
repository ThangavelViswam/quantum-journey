# Chapter 9: Dot Products & Duality

## Dot Product
- Multiply corresponding coordinates and add
- [a, b] · [c, d] = ac + bd
- Result is a single number (scalar)

## Geometric Interpretation
- Dot product = length of v × length of w × cos(angle between them)
- Positive → vectors point same direction
- Zero → vectors are perpendicular
- Negative → vectors point opposite directions

## Duality — The Deep Insight
- Every dot product is secretly a matrix multiplication
- A 1×2 matrix (row vector) applied to a 2D vector = dot product
- Duality between vectors and linear transformations onto a number line

## Projection Connection
- Dot product of v and w = projecting w onto v × length of v
- Symmetrical — order doesn't matter: v·w = w·v

## Quantum Computing Connection
- Dot products calculate probability amplitudes in quantum states
- Inner product of two quantum states = probability of one collapsing to the other
- Foundation of Dirac notation ⟨ψ|φ⟩ in Nielsen & Chuang
