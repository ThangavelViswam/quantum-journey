# Chapter 6: The Determinant

## Key Concepts
- Determinant measures how much a transformation scales area
  - det = 2 → doubles the area
  - det = 0.5 → squishes area to half
  - det = 0 → squishes space to a line or point

## Negative Determinant
- Flips orientation of space
- e.g. det = -2 → flips and doubles area

## det = 0 is Special
- Columns are linearly dependent
- Matrix has no inverse

## Formula for 2x2 Matrix
- det = ad - bc

## Quantum Computing Connection
- Quantum gates are unitary matrices with det = 1
- This preserves the size of quantum state vectors
